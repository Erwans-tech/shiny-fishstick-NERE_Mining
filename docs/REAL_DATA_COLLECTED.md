## Reserves & Resources (JORC Classified)
**Total Ore Reserves:** 33.2 Mt @ 0.89 g/t = 949,000 oz gold

### Deposits Breakdown (5 deposits):
1. **Goulagou 1 (GG1)** - Part of Karma permit
2. **Goulagou 2 (GG2)** - Part of Karma permit  
3. **Rambo** - Part of Karma permit
4. **Kao (North Kao)** - Separate permit, 262,000 oz added (2017)
5. **Nami** - Separate permit

### Measured & Indicated Resources:
- **Total:** 75.2 Mt @ 1.08 g/t Au = 2.6 Moz gold

## Mining Operations
- **Mining Method:** Open-pit, conventional truck and shovel
- **Material to Extract:** 113.8 Mt total (33.2 Mt to processing)
- **Mining Fleet:** 
  - 2x 200t hydraulic excavators
  - 1x Front-end loader
  - 4x 50t bulldozers
  - Up to 14x 90t trucks
  - 2x 300HP graders

## Processing
- **Technology:** Conventional heap leach process
- **Capacity:** 4.0 Mtpa (oxide & transition ore)
- **Process:** Crushing → Agglomeration → Cyanide leaching → CIC adsorption → Elution → Electrowinning → Smelting
- **Output:** Gold doré

## Project Finance (Historical)
- **Total Capital Expenditure:** $132 million
- **Funding Partners:** Franco-Nevada (75%, $75M) + Sandstorm (25%, $25M) = $100M
- **Gold Stream Obligation:** 100,000 oz over 5 years, then 6.5% of production
- **Payment Terms:** Up to $25M conditional on gold price

## Location & Infrastructure
- **Location:** 20km east of Ouahigouya, 185km NW of Ouagadougou
- **Permits:** Karma, Kao, Nami (3 exploitation permits)
- **Water Source:** Fresh water barrage on Nkambe River tributary = 1.3M m³/year
- **Power:** Local grid + diesel generators
- **Accommodation:** Ouahigouya (offsite)
- **Facilities:** Mine offices, safety/security, maintenance, assay lab, warehouse, medical room, sewage treatment
